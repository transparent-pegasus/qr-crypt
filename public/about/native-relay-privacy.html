QR Crypt Relay privacy information

Reviewed 2026-11-04. This notice describes the dedicated Windows relay under development. It does not describe the offline encryption PWA or establish that a native app has been released.

Never install or use the relay on a device that holds, or has ever held, QR Crypt keys, identities or plaintext. This includes a device with a browser where the offline QR Crypt app is installed. Use a separate online device. The camera sees everything in its view, not only the QR code. This app has not been independently audited.

Information the app handles

The relay converts QR message frames to text, text back to QR frames, or a single message frame to a QR image. It checks supported message formats but does not generate or store keys, encrypt, decrypt or authenticate messages. Exchange keys face to face, outside the relay.

When you start scanning, the app processes camera images to find QR codes. Those images can include people, documents and screens around the code. Text you type or paste enters the app before format validation, so accidentally entered personal information or secrets can also be processed. Do not enter them.

Accepted ciphertext still exposes message kind, stable shared-key or recipient-key identifiers, message size and frame count. Shared-key messages also expose the sender-reported creation time. Repeated identifiers can link transfers; encryption does not hide this information.

Storage and copying

Camera processing, entered text, collected frames and generated QR images use the current session's memory. The app does not intentionally save them to its persistent storage. Its only app-managed persistent setting is the language preference (oc-lang), written on initial display as well as when changed. The theme follows the operating system.

An explicit copy action writes text or a PNG to the system clipboard. The app does not read the clipboard through a clipboard-read API; ordinary typing and pasting provide input. Clipboard contents, history, synchronization and copies in a messenger remain outside the app's control after copying.

Closing the relay session, its time limit, leaving the page or the page becoming hidden ends the session, stops its camera tracks and releases app-held content. This is ordinary memory cleanup, not guaranteed erasure. The OS, WebView runtime, keyboard or input method, screenshots, swap and crash reporting may retain copies or other records. Closing or uninstalling the app does not establish that these copies have been erased. WebView profiles and caches are separate from the app's language setting.

Network and third parties

The reviewed relay code has no user account, analytics service or backend for sending message content. Its code and QR reader are bundled with it. You choose the messenger or other app into which you copy output; that service's handling, storage and synchronization apply to those copies.

This does not promise that the device, OS, WebView or Store never communicates or collects information. Their maintenance, diagnostics and distribution behavior are outside this app's control. A compromised device, runtime or app update can observe input and camera images and copy data. Store updates are allowed only on the separate relay device; the offline device must stay offline.

Your controls and questions

Camera use begins only after your explicit action and reader preparation. You can close the session or revoke camera access in the operating system. Copy only the intended output, keep unrelated people and sensitive material out of camera view, and manage clipboard history and synchronization in the OS and receiving apps. These actions do not guarantee deletion of existing copies.

For general, non-sensitive questions, use the project's public issue tracker. Do not post personal information, camera images, input text, keys or message ciphertext there. Report vulnerabilities through the private route in SECURITY.md; that route is for security reports, not a general confidential privacy inbox. A dedicated private privacy contact has not yet been published.
